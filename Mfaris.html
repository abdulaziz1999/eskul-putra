About santri

Being a santri means learning independence, discipline, and noble character. In a pesantren, we don't just pursue knowledge; we forge character and build lifelong brotherhood

Santri Day is celebrated every October 22nd

National Santri Day is celebrated in Indonesia every year on October 22. It was officially established by President Joko Widodo in 2015 to honor the significant role of Islamic students and scholars in the nation's history

This resolution called upon all Muslims to fight and defend Indonesia's newly declared independence against the returning Allied and Dutch forces. This spirit directly fueled the historic Battle of Surabaya on November 10

Santri also has it's own personalities during the National santri day

  • Participating in the Santri day ceremony
  • Praying and reciting shalawat together
  • Wearing traditional santri clothing
  • Respecting teachers and religious leaders
  • Joining religious activities
  • Showing discipline and religious attitudes
  • Taking part in social and community service activities
