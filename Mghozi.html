Something I want to be when I grow up

When I grow up, I want to be a soldier. Ever since I was little, I’ve been fascinated by the people in uniform who stand tall and brave. I see them on television during parades or helping during disasters, and I feel something inside me say, “I want to be like that.” To me, a soldier is not just someone who fights in battles. A soldier is someone who protects others, even when it is difficult or dangerous. They leave their families behind for long times so that people they don’t even know can live in peace. That kind of courage makes me admire them deeply. I know being a soldier is not easy. It requires discipline, strength, and a strong heart. It means waking up early, training hard, and never giving up even when you are tired. But I am ready to learn. I want to become stronger, both in my body and in my mind, so that one day I can serve my country with pride. My dream is to wear the uniform and stand tall, knowing that I am helping to keep people safe. I want my family to be proud of me, and I want to be someone others can depend on. I may still be young, but my dream is clear: I want to be a soldier who protects, serves, and never gives up.
